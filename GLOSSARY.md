# ComputerJy World

The language of the public site: how articles are presented, how readers move between them, and how the brand appears.

## Language

### Brand

**Site name**:
The full public name, "ComputerJy World".
_Avoid_: ComputerJy (that is the brand, not the site name)

**Brand accent**:
The letters "Jy", shown in the accent color wherever the site name appears in the logo.
_Avoid_: last two letters

**Look**:
The site's visual style: colors, fonts and spacing.
_Avoid_: theme (reserved for the WordPress theme)

### Homepage

**Lead block**:
The top of the first homepage page: one Lead card beside two Side cards.
_Avoid_: hero, featured section, lead-side

**Lead card**:
The largest card in the Lead block, for the newest article, and the only card with an excerpt.

**Side card**:
A smaller card in the Lead block, beside the Lead card.

**Feed**:
The grid of articles below the Lead block.
_Avoid_: primary feed, tile grid

**Feed card**:
One article in the Feed.
_Avoid_: tile, post card

**Card tags**:
The one to three tags shown on a card, chosen from the article's tags.
_Avoid_: category badge, chip

### Article page

**Share links**:
The controls a reader uses to send an article to another service or copy its address.
_Avoid_: social buttons, byline links

**Profile links**:
The links to ComputerJy's own accounts on other services, shown under Elsewhere in the footer.
_Avoid_: social links, share buttons

**Topic line**:
The labelled line of tag links at the end of an article.
_Avoid_: tag cloud

### Revenue

**Sponsor slot**:
A place on a page that shows sponsor content, and shows nothing when it has none.
_Avoid_: ad, banner
